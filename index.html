Glad you're here.

I started OG Ladka because most riders never get straight answers about their bikes. So I made this space — simple maintenance tips, honest gear reviews, and everything I wish someone had told me when I started riding.

Hello! I'm Hemansu Singh, a 22 y/o living in Bengaluru. My love for bikes started back in 2021 — my first ride was a Pulsar 150cc, obviously not mine, but my father's first bike. Somewhere between those rides, it stopped being just a machine. Slowly, I felt I was made for bikes. Today I proudly own a Classic 350, which I brought home in October 2025.

What you'll find here isn't just tips and reviews — it's everything I learned the hard way, so you don't have to. I break down what most people gatekeep, explain the "why" behind every bit of advice, and treat your bike like it's my own. If it saves you one bad decision or one wasted rupee, I've done my job.
